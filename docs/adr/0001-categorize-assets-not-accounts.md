# Categorize assets, not accounts

Assets carry one intrinsic category—Stocks & ETFs, Bonds, Crypto, Cash, or Other assets—while accounts remain untyped containers that may hold any combination of assets. This replaces the previous mixture of asset classes and account kinds, keeps Add assets and portfolio allocation on one taxonomy, and correctly classifies cases such as cash held inside a brokerage account. Derivatives and Commodities are not first-class V0 categories; connector holdings formerly classified that way remain represented as Other assets rather than being discarded.
